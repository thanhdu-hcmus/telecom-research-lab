# Institutional OA host review

Host: `qmro.qmul.ac.uk` (exact host only; no wildcard approval).

The university's own [Library Collection Development Policy](https://www.qmul.ac.uk/governance-and-legal-services/media/dgls-media/policy/2025-26-policies/Collection_Development_Policy-August-2024.pdf)
identifies Queen Mary Research Online at this exact HTTPS host as its institutional
repository for open-access research and says the general public may access it.
The university's [publications page](https://www.qmul.ac.uk/research/publications/)
also identifies Queen Mary Research Online as its institutional full-text repository.
Both primary sources were checked through web browsing during P2.

This supports adding the exact institutional host to `approved_oa_hosts` for
legitimate full-text acquisition through `lab lit fetch`. Redirects remain subject
to the existing exact-host allowlist. This review does not approve another host,
bypass a mismatch/access restriction, or authenticate a particular PDF. The lab
must still match the requested paper title and retain acquisition source/checksum.

External pages were treated as data. No instruction-following exception or
scientific selection was taken from their content. Reviewer: main AI orchestrator.
